## What Was Done

Added two layers of exception handling to `LoopGuardian.monitor()`:

1. **Outer try/except** wrapping the entire while-loop body (heartbeat checks, progress timeout checks, resource checks, and stale-loop killing). On unexpected error, logs via `logger.exception("guardian.monitor_error", ...)` and continues the loop.
2. **Inner try/except** wrapping each individual `kill_loop()` call inside the stale-loop iteration. On failure, logs via `logger.exception("guardian.kill_failed", loop_id=...)` and continues processing remaining stale loops.

The `await asyncio.sleep(self.HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL)` remains **outside** the outer try/except so the guardian always sleeps and retries regardless of errors.

## Design Decisions / Caveats

- `asyncio.sleep` placed **outside** the try/except: ensures the guardian always sleeps between ticks even if exception handling itself somehow fails (defensive).
- Inner kill-loop except is per-iteration: one loop's kill failure does not short-circuit the rest.
- `logger.exception` (not `logger.error`): captures full traceback for debugging production issues.
- Did **not** modify `kill_loop()` itself — that is STEP-FIX-04 scope (separate agent).
